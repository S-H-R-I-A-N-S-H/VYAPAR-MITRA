## What is Vyapar Mitra?

Vyapar Mitra is a multilingual, voice-first business companion designed to help first-time rural and semi-urban entrepreneurs make informed decisions before and after starting a business.

---

## Core Journey

### 1. Understand the Entrepreneur
The platform begins by understanding an entrepreneur's location, available capital, skills, interests, and business preferences, and identifies **3 to 5 potentially suitable business opportunities**.

### 2. Validate Through Hyper-Local Research
Each opportunity is validated through hyper-local market research covering demand, competitors, suppliers, pricing, market gaps, risks, and SWOT analysis.

Unlike generic AI recommendations, Vyapar Mitra combines **secondary research** with **primary market data** gathered through the entrepreneur's local network. The entrepreneur provides available responses through guided, AI-generated surveys covering consumers, competitors, suppliers, and retailers. Vyapar Mitra structures, analyses, and compares this data with its own research to identify important gaps or conflicts.

### 3. Financial Structuring
Vyapar Mitra answers the decisions that actually matter:
- Does the entrepreneur meet the defined eligibility conditions for a relevant loan or scheme?
- How much financing is actually required?
- How much can realistically be repaid within the chosen tenure?

It calculates the EMI and evaluates it against projected business cash flow, helping the entrepreneur understand whether the proposed borrowing is financially manageable **before taking on the debt**.

> **All critical financial calculations are performed using defined rules — not generated by AI.**

### 4. Detailed Project Report (DPR)
The business planning journey concludes with **automated DPR generation** — bringing market findings, business plan, and financial assessment into one structured document. The DPR can include:
- Proposed business and market findings
- Competition and primary research
- Project cost and financial projections
- Funding requirement and repayment information
- Relevant scheme information

### 5. Ongoing Business Support (In MVP)
Support does not stop once the business is started. The MVP also includes:

- **Offline-first digital logbook** — entrepreneurs record daily business information through voice-enabled logging, without extensive typing
- **Continuous AI insights** — analyses logbook records to identify whether the business is progressing as planned and highlights emerging issues or significant deviations
- **Guided issue discussion** — when an issue is identified, Vyapar Mitra discusses it through guided questions to understand the cause, then helps identify possible causes, suggests practical actions, and allows the entrepreneur to track whether the situation improves over time

---

## Accessibility

- **Multilingual voice interface** — easier to use for entrepreneurs uncomfortable with extensive typing or English business terminology
- **Offline-first approach** — entrepreneurs can record business information even with limited connectivity, with synchronisation when connectivity returns

---

## Guiding Principle

> **AI explains; deterministic code decides.**

AI handles language understanding, research, summarisation, and explanation. Critical financial calculations remain controlled and testable.

## MVP Scope

The MVP covers the **complete entrepreneur journey**:

```
Understand → Discover → Validate → Finance → Plan → Start → Record → Identify → Discuss → Improve → Grow
```

## Post-MVP Roadmap

The post-MVP phase adds deeper knowledge support and specialised compliance assistance:

1. **Business-Specific Knowledge Layer** — Gathers relevant knowledge from YouTube/podcast transcripts, articles, blogs, and research papers. Presents it to the entrepreneur in a summarised form in their preferred language, with actionable insights to support learning and application. This growing knowledge base helps entrepreneurs make better decisions and supports business scaling.

2. **GST Assistance and Tax Guidance** — ITR and tax guidance to help entrepreneurs understand and perform specific financial and compliance tasks as their business becomes more established.
